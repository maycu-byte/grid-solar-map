    Method and limits

    Data. Every operating solar unit in the state from Germany's official power plant register (Marktstammdatenregister), and the 110 kV substations mapped in OpenStreetMap.

    Location. Larger units have exact coordinates. Small rooftop units are published without them for privacy, so they are placed at the centre of their municipality.

    Grid areas. The real grid topology is not public. Each unit is assigned to its nearest substation (Voronoi partition), a common first approximation, not the operator's actual feeder layout.

    Use. This shows where solar concentrates and grows fastest — a first screen for where grid reinforcement studies would start, not a hosting-capacity calculation.
